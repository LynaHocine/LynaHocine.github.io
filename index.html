About Me

I am an M2 student in Algorithms, Artificial Intelligence, Interactions and Decision (AI2D) at Sorbonne University.

I am particularly interested in social robotics and in understanding how robots and AI can interact in meaningful and natural ways. I want to learn more about how robots can understand and adapt to human needs, collaborate with us and ultimately become useful companions or assistants in our daily lives.

My interests have led me toward Human-Robot Interaction, Interactive Robot Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Multi-Agent Systems. I am eager to explore these areas further and to contribute to the developement of robotic systems that can learn from and alongside people.
